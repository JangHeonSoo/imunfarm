Many owners think about selling farmland toward year-end: land inherited from parents, or fields being scaled back. In Korea, farmland the owner lived near and farmed personally for eight years can be fully exempt from capital gains tax, but the conditions are strict.

> Bottom line: <strong>farmland personally farmed for eight years while living nearby is 100% exempt from capital gains tax, capped at KRW 100 million of tax per year and KRW 200 million over five years.</strong> You must live in the same or an adjoining city/county/district, or within 30 km in a straight line. Years in which business income plus salary reached KRW 37 million do not count. The exemption must be applied for. Confirm your case with the tax office or a tax accountant.

## 1. Conditions

| Condition | Current rule                                                        | Basis              |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Residence | Same or adjoining si/gun/gu, or within 30 km straight-line distance | Decree Art. 66(1)  |
| Farming   | Regularly engaged, or at least half of the farm work by own labor   | Decree Art. 66(13) |
| Period    | Eight years in total meeting both                                   | Act Art. 69(1)     |
| Exclusion | Years with business income + salary of KRW 37 million or more       | Decree Art. 66(14) |

## 2. Caps

Article 133 limits the exempt tax to KRW 100 million per tax year and KRW 200 million across the current and previous four years. Older online posts citing KRW 200 million/300 million refer to other exemptions or outdated rules.

## 3. When the exemption shrinks or disappears

Land rezoned as residential, commercial or industrial is exempt only for gains up to the rezoning date, and loses the exemption three years after rezoning (with exceptions such as county areas of metropolitan cities). The same three-year rule applies to land designated for replotting.

## 4. Inherited land

The parent's farming period is added if the heir farms the land for at least one year while living nearby, or sells it within three years of inheritance even without farming it.

## 5. Documents and deadlines

Keep the farmland ledger (formerly nongjiwonbu), resident registration address history, farm business registration certificate, direct-payment records, receipts for fertilizer, pesticide and machinery, sales records and co-op membership. The preliminary return is due within two months from the end of the month of sale; the final return runs May 1–31 of the following year. The exemption application is filed with the return. The exempt amount is also free of the special tax for rural development, and land that fails residence or farming tests may be treated as non-business land taxed at 16–55%.
