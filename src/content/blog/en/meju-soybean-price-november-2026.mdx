Mid-November is when households that make their own soy sauce and doenjang order soybeans for meju. Here is what changed since October.

> Bottom line: <strong>On November 13, 2026, KAMIS domestic white soybeans (top grade, 40 kg) averaged 225,200 won across five cities, about 5,630 won per kg.</strong> That is 1.5% lower than October 8 and 1.8% lower than a year earlier. Online, products checked in October kept the same price, and a new-crop 5 kg bag sold for 29,900 won (5,980 won per kg), almost the wholesale price.

Earlier record: [Meju soybean prices 2026](/blog/meju-soybean-price-2026/).

The gap between Daegu and Busan widened from 20,000 won to 37,000 won per 40 kg. Imported white soybeans (35 kg) averaged 127,200 won, about 3,630 won per kg. See [domestic](/prices/soybean/) and [imported](/prices/soybean-white/) soybean prices.

## Online prices

Hyundai Nongsan 2 kg stayed at 13,500 won and Maisan 2 kg (2025 crop) at 15,900 won. A 2026-crop 5 kg bag was 29,900 won (5,980 won per kg), while some 1 kg packs cost 9,900 to 16,900 won. Pack size matters more than crop year.

## Making meju

Meju is traditionally made from the eleventh lunar month, around December. The Ministry of Food and Drug Safety recommends shaping blocks of about 1–2 kg and fermenting them below 30°C and 60% humidity. Soak the beans at least 10 hours, simmer at least 2 hours until soft, mash, shape, dry 1–2 weeks and ferment 2–3 weeks.

## Rules of thumb

- Buyers: decide how many kg of dry beans you will cook this winter. Above 5 kg, a new-crop 5 kg bag is the cheapest option. At 10 kg, the difference versus 1 kg packs is about 40,000 won.
- Growers: market choice beats timing. Daegu and Busan differed by 37,000 won per 40 kg on the same day. For direct sales, buyers will compare with the 29,900 won online 5 kg bag. Seoul prices from November to December fell or held in three of the last four years.
