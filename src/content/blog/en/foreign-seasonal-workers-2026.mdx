Planting and harvest arrive all at once, and local help is getting harder to find. More Korean farms are turning to the foreign seasonal worker program.

> Bottom line: <strong>Farms apply at their township office, and after a Ministry of Justice allocation review can employ workers for up to 8 months.</strong> 117,113 workers were allocated in 2026. Pay must be at least the minimum wage, 10,700 won an hour in 2027. Housing in greenhouses, containers or converted warehouses is not allowed. Small farms can use public seasonal work, where NongHyup employs the workers and sends them by the day; press reports put the farm's cost at about 100,000 won a day.

## 1. The program

The Ministry of Justice runs the E-8 seasonal work visa for up to 8 months. Workers come through agreements between Korean local governments and foreign ones, or as invited relatives of marriage migrants. Since January 23, 2026, only the state, local governments and designated agencies may recruit; illegal brokers face up to 3 years in prison or a 30 million won fine.

## 2. Allocations

| Item                      | Workers |

| 2026 first allocation     | 109,100 |
| 2026 second-half addition |  16,915 |
| 2026 total                | 117,113 |
| 2025                      |  95,596 |

The 2025 desertion rate was 0.5%, down from 1.6% in 2024.

## 3. How to apply and when

Farms registered as agricultural businesses apply at their township office; immigration pre-screens; a Ministry of Justice committee fixes numbers per city or county; visas are issued; workers arrive. Up to 9 workers per employer depending on farm size. For 2027, several counties took applications in August–September 2026 with review through November. The national 2027 first-half result had not been announced as of November 15, 2026; check your county notice.

## 4. Pay and insurance

| Year | Hourly minimum wage | Monthly (209 h) |
| ---- | ------------------: | --------------: |
| 2026 |          10,320 won |   2,156,880 won |
| 2027 |          10,700 won |   2,236,300 won |

Employers guarantee pay for at least 35 hours a week and provide room and board. Three mandatory insurances (wage-arrears guarantee, farmer safety insurance, worker accident insurance) carry fines after February 14, 2027.

## 5. Housing

Required: bedroom, toilet and shower, kitchen, heating and cooling, locks and fire safety. Banned: housing inside greenhouses, containers and converted warehouses, and since the second half of 2026 sites with heavy noise or odour or flood and landslide risk. Inspections run twice a year from 2026.

## 6. Public seasonal work

NongHyup branches employ workers and dispatch them to farms by the day. 130 branches with 4,739 workers were selected for 2026 (142 sites and 5,039 workers including local-government sites). The farm cost reported by the press is about 100,000 won per worker per day.
