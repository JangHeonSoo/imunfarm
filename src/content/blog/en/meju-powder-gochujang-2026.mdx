Plenty of households that never make meju still make gochujang, and they buy meju powder for it. Online you find "for gochujang", "for doenjang" and "for makjang" versions, and domestic 1kg bags range from the mid-10,000s to the mid-20,000s of won. Here is what the differences are and what it costs this season.

> Bottom line: <strong>Buy meju powder labeled for gochujang if you are making gochujang.</strong> Traditional gochujang meju mixes soybeans with rice, unlike soybean-only doenjang meju (Encyclopedia of Korean Culture). On November 28, 2026, domestic meju powder cost KRW 21,100 to 25,000 per kg on NH Mall and KRW 16,500 to 21,000 in a Coupang search. The KAMIS wholesale price of domestic white soybeans on November 27 was about KRW 5,580 per kg. A Sunchang ratio published by Korea Policy Briefing uses 2kg glutinous rice flour, 1kg meju powder and 3kg chili powder.

Related: [How to Make Meju 2026](/blog/meju-how-to-make-2026/).

## 1. What meju powder is

Korea's Food Code defines traditional meju as soybeans steamed or boiled, shaped and fermented, and improved meju as soybeans fermented with selected starter cultures. Meju powder is dried meju ground into flour. Gochujang is made from legumes or grains with at least 6% red pepper powder.

## 2. Gochujang, doenjang and makjang types

| Type      | Made from                                                          | Used for                  |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- |
| Gochujang | Soybeans mixed with rice (Sunchang: 1 mal rice to 8 doe soybeans)  | Gochujang                 |
| Doenjang  | Soybeans only; traditional meju serves both soy sauce and doenjang | Doenjang, soy sauce       |
| Makjang   | Raw meju ground to powder; Gyeongsang uses soybean and rice meju   | Makjang, a quick doenjang |

We found no official source saying makjang powder is made from soybeans and barley. Barley appears as cooked barley mixed in when making makjang in Chungcheong. Check the ingredient list.

## 4. Origin labels and storage

Meju, doenjang, gochujang and soybean flour have been targets of the agriculture ministry's origin-labeling crackdowns. False labels can bring up to seven years in prison or a KRW 100 million fine; missing labels, a fine of up to KRW 10 million. Check the origin field in the product details. We found no official storage rule for meju powder itself; the Rural Development Administration advises refrigerating meju (0–10°C) or vacuum packing it. Keep opened powder in an airtight container in the fridge or freezer.

## 5. Gochujang ratios

- Korea Policy Briefing, Sunchang (Feb 12, 2026): 5L water, 500g malt powder, 2kg glutinous rice flour, 1kg meju powder, 3kg chili powder, 1.5kg salt, 1 cup cheongju.
- Seoul Media Hub (Mar 20, 2025): 1kg glutinous rice, 1.5kg chili powder, 500g meju powder, 500g salt, 4L water, 500g malt; ferment 40–50 days in a crock.

Both use glutinous rice to meju powder at about 2 to 1.

## 6. For farms

With soybeans at about KRW 5,580 per kg wholesale, domestic meju powder sells for KRW 16,500 to 25,000 per kg. The gap covers one to two months of making, fermenting and drying meju, milling and small packaging. If you sell powder, label the type and every ingredient's origin, including rice.
