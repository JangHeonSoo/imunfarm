When we covered this law in August, the most common questions were how many years a project could run and how much farming it required. The law itself only said "within 30 years, as set by decree" and "farming must continue." On September 16 the Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs published draft enforcement decree and rules, and the numbers appeared for the first time. The law takes effect on December 17, 2026.

> Bottom line: <strong>The law's December 17 start is fixed; the decree and rules are still drafts.</strong> Under the drafts, a permit starts at up to 5 years and can be extended up to 3 years at a time, with extensions totaling up to 18 years, for a maximum of 23. At least 80% of the site must actually be farmed, shading must stay under 30%, structures must be at least 2.5 m high with posts at least 4 m apart. Farmers need three years of local residence, with an exception for young farmers. The cabinet meeting is scheduled for December 8, so check the final numbers then. This post reflects November 23, 2026.

> Earlier record: [Korea agrivoltaics law as of August 2026](/blog/agrivoltaic-law-august-2026/).

## 1. What changed since August

| Item                | August               | November 23                                              |
| ------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Decree and rules    | Only a drafting plan | Draft announced Sept 16; cabinet Dec 8 scheduled         |
| Project term        | Law: within 30 years | Draft: 5 years + 3-year extensions, max 23 years         |
| Farming duty        | Law: farm every year | Draft: at least 80% of the site cultivated               |
| Structure standards | Undecided            | Draft: shading under 30%, 2.5 m height, 4 m post spacing |
| Farmer residence    | To be set            | Draft: 3 years, young farmers exempt                     |
| Farmland Act rules  | –                    | Amendment notice Oct 13 to Nov 23                        |

## 2. What the law already fixes

Act No. 21804, promulgated June 16, 2026. Projects may be sited on farmland outside agricultural promotion zones or in designated renewable energy districts (Art. 6); resident cooperatives may go elsewhere after consulting the ministry (Art. 9). Eligible operators are local farmers with three years of farming, resident cooperatives of 10 or more, and farm corporations inside renewable energy districts (Art. 5). Mayors and county heads decide within 30 days, resident consultation is required and tenant farmers need the owner's consent (Art. 8). The permit counts as a temporary farmland-use permit (Art. 10). Violations can bring corrective orders, revocation, restoration orders and fines of up to 500 million won or three times the gain (Arts. 14–16). Tenants' renewal requests cannot be refused and rent increases are capped at 5% (Art. 18).

## 3. Application sequence (law and drafts)

1. Public notice of at least 14 days, plus a briefing session for projects of 300 kW or more, 10,000 m² or more, or when 20 residents request one.
2. Electricity business license under the Electricity Business Act.
3. Agrivoltaic permit application to the city or county, with temporary farmland-use documents.
4. Ministry consultation for cooperatives using other farmland.
5. Permit issued.

## 4. Still undecided

Final decree and rules, the final Farmland Act permit term, crop-specific yield standards, any agrivoltaic REC weighting (reported as "under review" by Electronic Times on November 7, 2026; no government source found), dedicated support programs, and the designated support center.
