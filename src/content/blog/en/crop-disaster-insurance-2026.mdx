Korea's crop disaster insurance (nongjakmul jaehae boheom) covers crop losses from natural disasters such as typhoons, hail, frost and drought. The government and local authorities pay most of the premium.

> Bottom line: <strong>Garlic and onion are insured in October–November, wheat and barley in November–December, at local NongHyup branches.</strong> The national government pays about 50% of the net premium and local governments add 15–40%. Exact sales dates are announced locally.

## 1. Scope and subsidy

The 2026 programme covers 78 crops (94 products); cucumber and greenhouse perilla leaves were added. NH NongHyup Non-Life Insurance sells it and the Agricultural Policy Insurance and Finance Service (APFS) manages it. For apples, pears, sweet and astringent persimmons and rice, the national share varies from 65% to 33% with coverage level. The national subsidy is capped at 50 million won per policyholder per product.

## 2. Sign-up windows (2026 guidelines)

| Crop                    | Window                  |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Garlic                  | Oct–Nov (rider Aug–Oct) |
| Onion                   | Oct–Nov                 |
| Wheat, barley           | Nov–Dec                 |
| Apple, pear, persimmons | Feb–Mar                 |

The October news about garlic and onion "insurance sales" referred to farm income stabilisation insurance; for the same crop you can join only one of the two.

## 3. Deductibles and 2026 changes

Most field crops and the four fruits allow 10, 15, 20, 30 or 40% deductibles. In 2026 the discount/surcharge bands grew from 15 to 35 (from 30% off to 50% surcharge), an accident-score system was added, unforeseeable extreme events are excluded from surcharges, and facility discounts now cover 25 crops.

## 4. Claims

Notify the insurer, loss assessment (loss adjusters assess damage rate), verification, decision, claim, and payment within 7 days of the claim; an advance of 50% of the estimated payout is possible. Do not clear damaged crops before assessment.

## 5. Related insurance

Farmer safety insurance covers farmers' injuries during farm work (NH NongHyup Life). Livestock disaster insurance and farm machinery insurance are separate, each with a 50% national subsidy (70% for small farms on machinery).
