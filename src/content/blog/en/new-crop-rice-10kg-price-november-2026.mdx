Most rice shoppers in Korea face the same question: two 10 kg bags or one 20 kg bag? Putting everything on a per-kilogram basis answers it quickly.

> Bottom line: <strong>On November 18, 2026, KAMIS recorded top-grade rice at 62,000 won per 20 kg wholesale in Seoul, or 3,100 won per kg.</strong> That is about 31,000 won for 10 kg. Seoul is 4.4% higher than a year ago (59,400 won), but the five-city average (59,640 won) is 0.9% lower. Online, the per-kg gap between 10 kg and 20 kg bags was small.

> Earlier: the same prices expressed per "mal" (8 kg) are in [Rice Price per Mal 2026](/blog/rice-one-mal-price-2026/), and home storage is covered in [Rice Storage Containers 2026](/blog/rice-storage-container-2026/).

Only Seoul rose meaningfully, from 59,000 won in mid-October to 62,000 won in November. The other cities barely moved and sit slightly below last year. These are prices from intermediate wholesalers to retailers. Daily figures are on the [rice price page](/prices/rice/).

## 2. New-crop rice

KAMIS tracks new-crop rice separately, but there has been no new reading since November 4. The last values (Seoul 62,000 won, Daegu 61,000 won, Busan 60,000 won, Daejeon 58,300 won, Gwangju 57,600 won) are within 0 to 1,000 won of regular rice on November 18. Seoul is 5% below the first new-crop reading a year earlier (65,400 won). History is on the [new-crop rice page](/prices/new-crop-rice/).

## 3. Farmgate price

Statistics Korea's farmgate rice price for November 5, 2026 was 229,612 won per 80 kg, down from 235,212 won on October 5. That is about 28,700 won per 10 kg, roughly 8% below the Seoul wholesale equivalent. According to reports citing Statistics Korea and the agriculture ministry, the 2026 rice crop is 3.508 million tonnes, 0.9% smaller than last year, on 670,000 ha (down 1.1%).

The 20 kg bag was only 49 won per kg cheaper. Milled rice loses flavor over time, so buying what you can eat in one to two months matters more than a few dozen won per kilogram.

## 5. What to read on the bag

Korean rice bags must show the production year, milling date and grade (special, high or normal). "Uninspected" labels are no longer allowed. The National Agricultural Products Quality Management Service is running a labeling crackdown from September 28 to November 30, 2026, checking production year, milling date, origin and variety.

## 6. For growers selling 10 kg bags

Searches for "10 kg rice" (64,600 a month) are almost as common as "20 kg rice" (69,000). Use the farmgate equivalent (about 28,700 won per 10 kg) as a floor and the wholesale equivalent (28,800 to 31,000 won) as a benchmark. Keep the per-kg gap between 10 kg and 20 kg small, and include every required label item.

## 7. Conclusion

Mid-November wholesale rice is 62,000 won per 20 kg in Seoul and 59,640 won on average across five cities, or about 30,000 won per 10 kg. Choose bag size by how fast you eat, not by price, and check the production year, milling date and grade.
