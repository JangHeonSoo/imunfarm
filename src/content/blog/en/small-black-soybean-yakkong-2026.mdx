Search for black soybeans in Korea and seoritae always comes with a smaller neighbor: jwinunikong. Both are black, both say "domestic," yet one is sold for rice and the other for roasting and cheonggukjang. They are also priced differently. Here is what separates them.

> Bottom line: <strong>Jwinunikong, also called seomoktae or yakkong ("medicine bean"), is a small-seeded black soybean.</strong> A provincial extension service classes it as a small-seeded type at about 10 g per 100 seeds, less than half of large-seeded beans (about 25 g). On Coupang on October 26, 2026, domestic jwinunikong sold for roughly KRW 9,000 to 15,000 per kilogram. We found no harvest date specific to jwinunikong, so growers follow the general soybean rule: harvest 7 to 14 days after 95% of pods turn color.

## 1. What it is

The name means "rat's-eye bean," after its small black seeds. In Chinese characters it is seomoktae, and because it was used in traditional medicine it is also called yakkong. The Rural Development Administration (RDA) describes it as a bean used for meju and soy sauce, ground soy milk soup and mixed rice; today it also goes into soy milk, puffed snacks and grain powders. Most beans on the market are local landraces rather than registered varieties.

## 2. Jwinunikong vs seoritae

| Bean        | Other names        | Seed size (100 seeds) | Common uses                       |
| ----------- | ------------------ | --------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Jwinunikong | Seomoktae, yakkong | Small (about 10 g)    | Roasting, cheonggukjang, soy milk |
| Seoritae    | Sokcheong          | Large                 | Rice, soy milk, braised beans     |
| Heuktae     | Black bean         | Large                 | Rice, braised beans               |

Jeju's agricultural extension service groups soybeans as small (about 10 g per 100 seeds, sprout-type, including jwinunikong), medium (about 17 g) and large (about 25 g). Seoritae is on the large side; Gyeongbuk's black soybean 'Bitnadu' weighs 40.5 g per 100 seeds.

On health claims: RDA notes isoflavones, lecithin, polyphenols, anthocyanins and fiber. A 2020 RDA–KIST study on 'SCEL-1', a separate small landrace line (9.4 g per 100 seeds), reported benefits in mice only, and its seed is grown only under contract. That is not evidence for ordinary market beans.

## 4. Roasting at home

Rinse and remove floaters, dry the beans completely, then stir them in a dry pan over low heat until the skins crack and they smell nutty. Bite one; if crisp through, cool them on a tray before sealing in a container.

## 5. Growers: harvest and drying

| Step         | Standard                                                       | Source          |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------- |
| Maturity     | Leaves dropped, 95%+ of pods turned their variety color        | RDA (2026)      |
| Harvest      | About 7–14 days after maturity, pod moisture 15–18%            | RDA (2026)      |
| Late harvest | Shattering 0.1% on time, 5.0% at 20 days late, 9.4% at 40 days | RDA (2022)      |
| Drying       | Slowly below 30°C; 30–35°C for processing, 25–30°C for seed    | RDA (2022/2019) |
| Storage      | About 14% moisture (about 12% for seed)                        | RDA (2022)      |

Small seeds slip through coarse sieves, so use a finer screen when cleaning. Standard seeding for small-seeded types is 4–5 kg per 10 ares. With no wholesale quote, online retail prices are the practical reference for direct sales.
