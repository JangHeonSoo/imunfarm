Ask anyone about starting to farm in Korea and the first question is whether you have registered as an agricultural business entity (nongeop gyeongyeongche). Tax-free fuel, machinery rental and storage subsidies all ask for the registration certificate first.

> Bottom line: <strong>you can register if you farm at least 1,000㎡ yourself, or, on less land, can prove at least KRW 1.2 million in annual sales.</strong> Apply at the local office of the National Agricultural Products Quality Management Service (NAQS) with your farmland ledger and a farming confirmation form. After a field check it takes 30 days (90 days if you also apply for public-interest direct payments). There is no fee. Registration is valid for 3 years and changes must be registered. The certificate is free and instant on Gov24.

## 1. What registration is

Article 4 of the Act on Fostering and Supporting Agricultural and Fishery Business Entities requires registration to receive government loans and subsidies. The agriculture ministry lists direct payments, pension and health insurance support, state subsidies and loans, and tax relief as linked benefits (February 2024). A registration is valid for 3 years from registration or change registration.

## 2. Requirements

Under Article 3 of the Enforcement Decree of the Framework Act on Agriculture, Rural Community and Food Industry, a farmer is someone who farms at least 1,000㎡ of farmland, sells at least KRW 1.2 million of farm produce a year, or works in farming at least 90 days a year. The Farmland Act decree adds facilities such as a fixed greenhouse of 330㎡ or more. Since February 2024, registration also requires a legitimate right to the land (ownership or lease) and real farming activity such as sales or input purchases. Since October 2024, land under 1,000㎡ requires proof of at least KRW 1.2 million in sales.

## 3. How to apply

Apply at the NAQS office for your address by visit, mail or fax; if you also apply for direct payments, the town office also accepts it. Documents: farmland ledger, farming confirmation form, a lease contract if the lease is not in the ledger, and purchase or sales receipts on request. Steps: submission, document review, system check, field inspection, registration. Processing takes 30 days (90 with direct payments), with no fee. We could not confirm whether a first registration can be completed fully online through Nongup-eZ.

## 4. Farmland ledger and certificate

The farmland register (nongjiwonbu) became a parcel-based farmland ledger (nongjidaejang) on April 15, 2022, issued via Gov24. Lease changes must be reported within 60 days. The registration certificate is free and instant on Gov24, at NAQS or town offices, or at unmanned kiosks.

## 5. After registering

- Register changes in land, crops or area within 14 days. From 2026, failing to do so cuts public-interest direct payments by 10%.
- Registration expires after 3 years; since January 2026 you can re-register within a year by proving KRW 1.2 million in sales.
- False registration: fine of up to KRW 5 million and no re-registration for a year. Refusing a field check: administrative fine up to KRW 1 million.

## 6. Weekend farms

The Farmland Act limits weekend or hobby farmland to under 1,000㎡ per household, so such land alone cannot meet the area test; you would need to meet the sales test. This is our reading of the rules, not an official statement, so ask your NAQS office first.
