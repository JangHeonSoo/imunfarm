Half a bag of new-crop red beans left after a pot of patjuk is the right amount for yanggaeng. The ingredients are simple; the trouble is agar.

> Bottom line: <strong>the RDA Nongsaro recipe uses 1kg red bean paste, 22g agar, 500g water, 150g brown sugar and 60g corn syrup, and the same page makes the paste from 500g dried beans.</strong> Soak the agar 2–3 hours and boil until no grains remain or it will not set. On November 6, 2026, domestic red beans sold for KRW 724,000 per 40kg in Seoul (KRW 18,100/kg, KAMIS).

## 1. Paste from 500g of beans

Nongsaro: 500g red beans, 200g sugar, 1 tsp salt. Soak overnight (at least 3 hours), boil once and discard the water, simmer in fresh water until very soft, add sugar and salt, boil again, sieve out the skins and cool. About an hour of cooking. A 2011 National Institute of Crop Science study measured wet paste yields of 188–205% across varieties, which is roughly 1kg of paste from 500g of dry beans.

## 2. Agar and setting

A 1997 study in the Korean Journal of Food and Nutrition found agar starts dissolving at 80°C, needs much higher temperatures to dissolve fully, and gels at roughly 36–41°C. Hence Nongsaro's steps: soak the agar in its water for 2–3 hours, boil over medium heat while stirring until no grains remain, dissolve the sugar, turn off the heat and loosen the paste in, warm again, add syrup (and nuts if using), boil once more, pour into molds and chill.

## 3. Yield and cost

All ingredients total a little over 1.7kg before evaporation; at 50g per piece that is roughly 30 pieces (our estimate). Beans dominate the cost: 500g of domestic red beans is about KRW 9,050 at Seoul wholesale versus about 3,340 for imported (KRW 267,000 per 40kg, medium grade). Domestic red beans are 11.7% cheaper than a year earlier (820,000).

## 4. Storage

Nongsaro gives no setting time or fridge life, only: freeze for long storage and eat as soon as possible. Keep a few days' worth chilled and wrap the rest individually to freeze.

## 5. For farmers

In a year when red bean prices are down over 10%, paste or yanggaeng adds value, but selling processed food may require food manufacturing or on-site processing registration; ask the local hygiene office first. A "500g for one batch of yanggaeng" bag is a simpler start. Compare market returns with the [produce market calculator](/tools/produce-market-calculator/?item=red-bean).
