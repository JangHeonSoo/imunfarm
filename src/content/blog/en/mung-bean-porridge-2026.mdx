Mung bean porridge is a classic Korean winter and recovery food. It is easy once you know the soaking time and the ratio.

> Bottom line: <strong>Soak hulled mung beans 7–8 hours in winter (3–4 hours in summer), then simmer equal amounts of hulled beans and soaked rice in plenty of water.</strong> Nongsaro's recipe is 3 tbsp hulled mung beans, 3 tbsp soaked rice and 3 cups water. On November 13, 2026, KAMIS domestic mung beans (top grade) were 500,000 won per 40 kg in Seoul, about 12,500 won per kg.

> Earlier notes: [Domestic Hulled Mung Bean Prices 2026](/en/blog/domestic-mung-bean-price-2026/) and [Mung Bean Pancakes 2026](/en/blog/mung-bean-pancake-2026/).

## 1. Hulled or whole

Hulled (split, yellow) mung beans cook faster and need no peeling. For whole beans, KAMIS suggests soaking about 8 hours and rubbing off the skins. Alternatively boil them soft and press through a sieve.

## 2. Ratio

| Hulled mung beans | Soaked rice |  Water | Servings |
| ----------------: | ----------: | -----: | -------: |
|            3 tbsp |      3 tbsp | 3 cups |      1–2 |
|            6 tbsp |      6 tbsp | 6 cups |      3–4 |

1 cup = 200 ml, 1 tbsp = 15 ml. The traditional Chungcheong style in the Encyclopedia of Korean Culture uses about 2 parts mung beans to 1 part rice and boils the beans in 8 times their volume of water.

## 3. Cooking

Put beans, rice and water in a heavy pot, simmer on low heat and stir so it does not stick. When the rice grains break down, season with salt. No official cooking time is given. We found no official guideline for pressure cookers or rice-cooker porridge settings. Cool leftovers quickly, refrigerate within 2 hours (general guidance from the food safety agency), and reheat thoroughly.

## 5. For growers

Mung beans are sown in June and picked by hand 3–4 times, starting 40–50 days after flowering (Nongsaro). Jeollanam-do has about 61.5% of the planted area (KAMIS). Hulled beans sell for more at retail, but hulling loses weight and costs milling fees; we found no official yield figure, so calculate with your own mill.
