October is when Korean households make saenggang-cheong, ginger preserved in sugar for tea. New-crop ginger is cheap this year, and homemade syrup costs a fraction of store-bought.

> Bottom line: <strong>On October 8, 2026, domestic ginger (grade A, 10 kg) averaged KRW 57,760 across five wholesale cities, about KRW 5,800 per kilogram, 27% below a year earlier.</strong> On October 12, 1 kg of domestic new-crop ginger cost KRW 7,800 online and 1 kg of sugar KRW 2,240. A 1:1 batch makes about 2 kg of syrup, a little over three 500 ml jars, for about KRW 10,000 in ingredients.

## 2. The ratio

Nongsaro (Rural Development Administration) makes ginger syrup by grating ginger, straining clear juice, adding sugar equal in weight to the juice, simmering gently and sealing it in a sterilized glass jar. The common home method layers sliced ginger with an equal weight of sugar, the same 1:1 fruit-to-sugar ratio the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety gives for plum syrup.

## 3. Yield and cost

1 kg of sliced ginger plus 1 kg of sugar weighs about 2 kg. Heavy sugar syrup has a specific gravity of roughly 1.3, so that is about 1.5 liters, or a little over three 500 ml jars.

## 4. Storing ginger before you make syrup

Nongsaro gives 12–14°C and 85–90% humidity as the storage range. Above 18°C ginger sprouts; below 10°C it suffers chilling injury and rots, so a regular fridge is a poor place for whole ginger. Make syrup within the week, or peel, slice and freeze the rest.

## 5. For growers

Nongsaro puts the ginger harvest at late October to early November, before frost for seed ginger. Small or misshapen ginger goes to grade B at wholesale, about KRW 10,000 less per 10 kg on October 8, but shape does not matter for syrup, so selling it directly as "syrup ginger" can pay. Making and selling syrup yourself requires a business report under the Food Sanitation Act: immediate sale manufacturing and processing for direct sale on site, or food manufacturing and processing for packaged distribution, plus mail-order registration for online sales. Check with the county sanitation office first.
