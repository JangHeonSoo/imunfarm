After kimchi-making season, Korean kitchens stock up on winter soup ingredients. A 1.5 kg box of large dried anchovies lasts months, and this year it is noticeably cheaper.

> Bottom line: <strong>On November 18, 2026, KAMIS recorded large dried anchovies at 12,600 won per 2 kg wholesale in Seoul, or 6,300 won per kg</strong>, 17.6% below a year ago. The five-city average is 11.5% lower. Seoul's November average fell from 25,600 won in 2023 to 12,600 won this year. Small anchovies, by contrast, are 26.5% more expensive in Seoul.

## 3. Size matters

On the same day in Seoul, large anchovies were 12,600 won per 2 kg, medium 14,300 won (–13.9%) and small 25,300 won (+26.5%). For stock, buy large; small anchovies for stir-frying cost about twice as much this year.

## 4. A 1.5 kg box

At wholesale, 1.5 kg is about 9,450 won in Seoul. On November 19, one 1.5 kg box of southern-coast stock anchovies on Coupang was 11,900 won, 26% above that. Look for domestic origin, a silvery color, no rancid smell and intact fish. Divide into portions and freeze.

## 5. Jukbang anchovies

Jukbang anchovies are caught in V-shaped bamboo weirs in the Jijok Strait, Namhae. The fishery was designated National Important Fishery Heritage No. 3 in 2015 and was reported as listed by the FAO as a Globally Important Agricultural Heritage System in July 2025. Small catches keep prices high.

## 6. Conclusion

Large dried anchovies are 6,300 won per kg wholesale in Seoul, 17.6% cheaper than last year and less than half the November 2023 level. A 1.5 kg box at around 10,000 won is close to wholesale.
