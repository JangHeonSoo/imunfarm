Pick a gimjang day too early and the kimchi sours fast; pick it too late and the cabbage and radish in the field freeze. Prices matter too, and this year fall cabbage reached the KAMIS survey nearly two weeks earlier than last year, at a much lower price.

> Bottom line: <strong>The best time for gimjang is when the daily mean temperature stays at or below 4°C and the daily low at or below 0°C.</strong> By normal values that is mid-November in Chuncheon, late November in Seoul, early December in Daejeon and Daegu, mid-December in Gwangju and early January in Busan. On October 30, 2026, fall napa cabbage (top grade, 10kg net of three heads) was KRW 9,400 in Seoul and KRW 8,338 on average across five cities, 39% below Seoul's average last November. In seven of the last ten years, December cabbage was cheaper than November.

## 1. The temperature rule

The Korea Meteorological Administration and private forecasters use the same rule: a daily mean of 4°C or lower and a daily low of 0°C or lower, sustained. Warmer, and kimchi ferments too fast. Colder, and cabbage and radish freeze. In practice, the weekend when your weekly forecast starts showing sub-zero lows is the window.

December was cheaper in seven of ten years, and the three rises were all under 4%. Within November last year, the first and second halves differed by only 3%. Set the date by temperature, then buy cabbage one to three days before. Buy chili powder, salted seafood and salt two to three weeks ahead, and garlic and ginger a week ahead.

## 5. For growers

Fall cabbage prices usually appear in late October or November and run through December, with winter cabbage joining in December in recent years. RDA's home-garden guide suggests harvesting about 60 days after transplanting, when the head gives 1–2cm under a press. Watch for sustained sub-zero lows, and match direct-sale delivery dates to the regional normal gimjang dates.
