## 1. Radish prices now

KAMIS wholesale, November 5, 2026, autumn radish top grade 20kg: Seoul KRW 12,300, Busan 11,000, Daegu 11,000, Gwangju 10,700, Daejeon 12,000. Any firm radish without internal pithiness works — including leftover kimchi radishes with minor surface blemishes.

## 2. How much dried radish you get

The Korean Food Composition Table (DB 10.4, 2026) lists fresh Korean radish at 93.8g water per 100g and dried radish at 14.5g. The 6.2% solids become dried strips at 14.5% moisture, so the yield is about 7%: 20kg of fresh radish gives roughly 1.4–1.5kg of mumallaengi; one 1.5kg radish gives about 100g. In exchange, 100g of dried radish carries 326mg calcium, 3,489mg potassium and 23.7g dietary fiber.

## 3. Cutting and drying

An RDA-affiliated study (Korean Journal of Food and Cookery Science, 2015) compared 3.5mm and 7.0mm cuts dried naturally versus hot-air drying at 45°C: hot-air drying browned less. Thinner cuts dry faster but break easily; thicker cuts keep more chew after soaking. Home cooks usually cut sticks about half a finger thick.

Sun-drying: spread the sticks on bamboo trays or a hanging mesh dryer without overlap, turn once or twice a day, bring them in at night. There is no fixed number of days — judge by state, not the calendar: they are done when they snap instead of bending. A food dehydrator is the fallback for wet weather and keeps the color whiter; home units draw around 250W, so even 10 hours uses only 2.5kWh.

## 4. Storage and soaking

Fully dried strips keep well if moisture is kept out: portion into zipper bags or airtight containers and store cool or frozen. For muchim (seasoned salad), soak briefly in lukewarm water just until the core softens — oversoaking washes out sweetness and texture — then squeeze and dress with gochugaru, soy sauce or fish sauce, garlic, rice syrup and sesame.

## 5. For growers

Radishes that missed the grading box are worth drying: at KRW 500–600 per kg wholesale, converting culls into a storable product cuts waste, and dried radish is light enough for parcel direct sales.
