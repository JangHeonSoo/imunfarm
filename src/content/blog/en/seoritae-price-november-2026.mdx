In early October most seoritae for sale online was from the 2025 crop. By mid-November, listings labeled "2026 new crop" have arrived. Did prices fall?

> Bottom line: <strong>On November 16, 2026, new-crop Korean seoritae sold online for 6,700 to 18,800 won per kg.</strong> A 5 kg bag was cheapest at 6,700 won per kg. Two 2025-crop products checked in October (15,980 won and 8,980 won per 1 kg) had not changed price. Old beans did not get cheaper, and new beans are not more expensive. Compare by crop year and price per kg.

Earlier record: [Seoritae harvest time and prices 2026](/blog/seoritae-black-soybean-harvest-2026/).

## Same products, one month later

Seoritae is not covered by KAMIS, so we rechecked the Coupang products used in the October 9 post (listed prices, account coupons excluded). Shinrim Nonghyup 1 kg (2025 crop) stayed at 15,980 won and Nonghyup Daehan Mingok 1 kg stayed at 8,980 won. One 2 kg product changed its option set and two others no longer appeared in search.

| Hand-sorted seoritae         | 8 kg (one _mal_) | 80,000 won | 10,000 won |
| Domestic seoritae            | 1 kg             | 18,800 won | 18,800 won |

A _mal_ is a volume unit, so its weight differs by seller. This seller used 8 kg. Always convert to price per kg.

## Wholesale reference

KAMIS domestic white soybeans (top grade, 40 kg, five-city average) were 225,200 won on November 13, 2026 (about 5,630 won per kg), down 1.5% from October 8 and 1.8% from a year earlier. Most of the drop came from Busan. See [soybean prices](/prices/soybean/).

## Rules of thumb

- Buyers: new-crop beans are now available. Buy about three months' worth. Rural Development Administration guidance says dry beans keep about three months in a cool, ventilated place at 18–22°C. Above 10,000 won per kg, look for a reason such as sorting or origin.
- Growers: buyers will compare your price with 6,700 won per kg for a 5 kg online bag. Selling for more requires showing your field, crop year and sorting quality. Seoul white soybean prices from November to December fell or held in three of the last four years, so waiting for a rise has weak support.
