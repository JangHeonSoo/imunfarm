Autumn leaves Korean kitchens full of bulk buys: a mal of perilla seed, new sesame, chili powder for kimchi, rice cakes for the New Year. A home vacuum sealer lets you split them into portions so only the bag you are using meets air and moisture.

> Bottom line: <strong>Vacuum packing keeps out air and moisture; it does not replace refrigeration or freezing.</strong> In a Korean national trial, chili powder grew the least mold at 10°C, and humidity below 69% was recommended. Home models are mostly external-suction types; cordless ones start around KRW 30,000. A 28cm x 5m roll pack (three rolls) costs about KRW 12,000, roughly KRW 240 per 30cm bag.

## 1. What vacuum packing does and does not do

Removing air reduces oxidation and keeps moisture out, and flat bags stack well in the freezer. But low oxygen suits some bacteria, notably Clostridium botulinum. The UK Food Standards Agency's 2008 guidance advised manufacturers to give chilled vacuum-packed foods no more than 10 days of shelf life unless other controls are in place. At home: what needs the fridge stays in the fridge, what needs the freezer stays in the freezer.

## 2. Chili powder: not necessarily the freezer

The National Institute of Agricultural Sciences stored chili powder and dried peppers for over ten months at temperatures from –20°C to 30°C. Chili powder had the least mold at 10°C; dried peppers at 0°C. At –20°C, 0°C and 4°C, mold rose sharply after a while. Humidity below 69% was recommended. Seal a month or two of powder at a time and keep it cool; store long-term supply as whole dried peppers and grind as needed.

## 3. Portioning guide

| Item                        | Portion           | Where                              |
| --------------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Whole perilla or sesame     | 500g–1kg          | Fridge or freezer, fully dry first |
| Ground perilla, sesame salt | 100–200g          | Freezer                            |
| Rice cakes                  | One meal          | Freezer, laid flat                 |
| Chili powder                | One to two months | Around 10°C                        |

## 4. Choosing a sealer

Home sealers usually draw air out at the bag opening and need embossed bags. Chamber sealers handle liquids and smooth bags but are bigger and pricier. Cordless suits occasional use; corded suits long sessions. "Wet/dry" modes help with slightly moist food, but liquids will damage an external-suction sealer, so freeze wet items first. Check seal length against roll width, cutter, power source and continuous-use rating.
