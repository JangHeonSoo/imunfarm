A bottle of sesame oil made from Korean-grown seed costs over KRW 30,000 at the supermarket. The plain "sesame oil" next to it costs a third of that. Almost all of the gap comes from the seed. So some households buy domestic seed and have it pressed at a local mill, while sesame growers ask whether to sell seed or oil. Both questions start from the price of 1 kg of seed and 1 ml of oil.

> Bottom line: <strong>In the KAMIS survey on October 12, 2026, domestic white sesame (top grade, 30 kg) averaged KRW 731,400 at wholesale across five cities, about KRW 24,400 per kilogram</strong>, 8.5% below a year earlier. Chinese sesame was about KRW 7,200 per kilogram, less than a third of domestic. On October 13, domestic sesame oil sold online for KRW 33,000–37,000 per 300–350 ml bottle, or KRW 100–117 per ml. Oil pressed from Chinese seed was KRW 38–65 per ml.

## 3. How much oil does 1 kg of seed give?

There is no official standard yield. Research on sesame collections at Korea's National Agrobiodiversity Center found crude fat content averaging 46.5–50.9% by weight (48.0% for Korean-bred varieties). If half of 1 kg is oil and sesame oil has a density of about 0.92, the theoretical maximum is around 540 ml, or 1.8 bottles of 300 ml. Real pressing leaves oil in the cake and loses moisture in roasting, so the actual yield is lower and varies by mill.

The break-even point can still be calculated:

| Seed cost per kg                    | To match KRW 100/ml | To match KRW 111/ml |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------: | ------------------: |
| Wholesale, KRW 24,400               |      244 ml or more |      220 ml or more |
| Retail, KRW 31,500 (Sep KAMIS avg.) |      315 ml or more |      284 ml or more |

Pressing fees, washing and bottles come on top. Before handing seed to a mill, ask how many ml per kilogram they usually get, what extra fees they charge, and whether you can choose the roast level.

## 4. How should sesame oil be stored?

Sesame oil keeps far better than perilla oil. According to Korea's Rural Development Administration (RDA), perilla oil is over 60% alpha-linolenic acid while sesame oil is under 1%. RDA recommends refrigerating perilla oil, but for sesame oil it advises a cool place away from direct sunlight with stable temperatures. Sesame oil is still 80% unsaturated fat, so keep the cap closed, use brown bottles, and split large batches into small bottles.

## 5. Sesame and perilla oil gift sets

Searches for sesame–perilla oil gift sets rise from Chuseok through the Lunar New Year. Sets use small bottles and packaging, so they cost more per ml; a domestic 180 ml gift set was KRW 25,190 (KRW 140/ml) on October 13. Check the origin of each oil separately, the bottle size, and the production date.

## 6. Should growers sell seed or oil?

| Sales route                | Reference value                         | Fits when                                          |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Collectors / wholesale     | Below KRW 24,400/kg wholesale           | Large volume, no time to sort and pack             |
| Direct sale of seed (1 kg) | Between wholesale and retail KRW 31,500 | Clean seed, harvest year can be guaranteed         |
| Pressed oil                | KRW 100–117/ml retail                   | Regular buyers, food business registration, labels |

Seoul monthly wholesale prices from October to December rose in only one of the last four years (2024, +9.6%), so waiting is no sure gain. Compare city payouts with the [produce market calculator](/en/tools/produce-market-calculator/?item=sesame).
