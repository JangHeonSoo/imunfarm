On kimchi-making day there is always a pile of tough, dark outer cabbage leaves. Most people throw them away. Those leaves are ugeoji, the greens that go into gamjatang and doenjang soup all winter. All they need is one blanching.

> Bottom line: <strong>ugeoji means the outer leaves picked off when trimming vegetables; siraegi means dried radish greens or cabbage leaves (Standard Korean Language Dictionary).</strong> Wash the outer leaves, blanch in lightly salted water, then freeze in single portions with a little water left in, or dry them in an airy, shaded spot. Per 100g, raw ugeoji has 141mg of calcium versus 40mg in raw autumn cabbage (Korean Food Composition Table). On November 4, 2026, autumn napa cabbage (top grade, 10kg) sold for KRW 9,400 wholesale in Seoul, or KRW 940 per kg (KAMIS).

## 1. Ugeoji vs siraegi

The dictionary defines ugeoji as "the outer stalks set aside when trimming greens" and siraegi as "dried radish greens or cabbage leaves." Ugeoji names the part; siraegi names the state. Dried outer cabbage leaves are both. In everyday use, blanched outer cabbage leaves are usually called ugeoji and dried radish greens siraegi.

## 3. Five steps

We found no official blanching time or salt amount specific to ugeoji, so we follow the Rural Development Administration (RDA) guidance for siraegi.

1. Sort out yellowed, soft or insect-damaged leaves.
2. Wash two or three times in a large basin.
3. Blanch in boiling water with a little salt (RDA: salt keeps the colour) until the thick stems bend easily.
4. Cool at once in cold water.
5. Split into portions to use now, freeze, or dry.

**Freezing:** RDA says boiled siraegi frozen "with a little water still in it" stays tender. Pack fist-sized portions in zipper bags and date them. We found no official freezer life.

**Drying:** RDA recommends a place that is "well ventilated and out of direct sunlight." Spread leaves one by one so they do not overlap, or they will mould. Soak dried leaves before cooking; RDA boils dried siraegi for 25–30 minutes in a pressure cooker.

## 5. For growers

Nongsaro lists autumn napa cabbage shipping from mid-October to early December, freeze damage at minus 3–4°C, and storage at 0–3°C with 95% humidity. RDA advises leaving frozen cabbage in the field until it thaws rather than harvesting at once.

Seoul monthly averages for autumn cabbage (10kg) were lower in December than in November in each of the last six years (2020: -33%, 2021: -15%, 2022: -21%, 2023: -14%, 2024: -6%, 2025: -5%). November, kimchi season, is usually the best month to sell.
