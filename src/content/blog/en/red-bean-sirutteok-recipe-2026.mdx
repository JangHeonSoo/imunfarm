When a Korean family moves house, opens a shop or holds an autumn household rite, a tray of red bean sirutteok appears. With the new red bean crop in, many people try steaming it at home and get stuck on the same points: how much bean to rice, how long to steam, and why the middle stays raw.

> Bottom line: <strong>the Rural Development Administration (RDA) Nongsaro recipe uses 3.6 kg non-glutinous rice flour, 1.4 kg red bean crumble, 2 tbsp salt and 200–400 mL water.</strong> Scaled to 1 kg of rice flour that is about 390 g of crumble (our calculation). Layer the flour 3 cm thick between crumble, cover once steam rises through the top, steam about 20 minutes and rest briefly. On November 11, 2026 domestic red beans were KRW 724,000 per 40 kg wholesale in Seoul, or KRW 18,100 per kg.

## 1. Ingredients

| Ingredient                   | Nongsaro amount  | For 1 kg rice flour (our calculation) |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Non-glutinous rice flour     | 3.6 kg           | 1 kg                                  |
| Red bean crumble (pat-gomul) | 1.4 kg (12 cups) | about 390 g                           |
| Salt                         | 2 tbsp           | about 1/2 tbsp                        |
| Water                        | 200–400 mL       | about 55–110 mL                       |

There is no sugar. Water is a range because wet-milled flour already holds moisture. We found no official figure for flour moisture; the recipe only says to rub water into the flour and sieve it.

## 2. Red bean crumble

Crumble is not paste; the beans are lightly pounded with skins on. From the Nongsaro glutinous sirutteok recipe (Suncheon, 2001):

1. Bring the beans to a boil and discard that first water.
2. Add about three times their volume of cold water and boil until soft.
3. When nearly done, pour off the water and let them steam dry over low heat.
4. Add salt and pound lightly.

The Encyclopedia of Korean Culture adds that the water should be poured off when the beans are about 80% cooked, otherwise the crumble turns wet. We found no official yield from dry beans to crumble.

## 3. Layering and steaming

1. Spread a thick layer of crumble on the steamer base.
2. Spread rice flour 3 cm thick without pressing.
3. Repeat, finishing with crumble.
4. Set over boiling water. When steam comes through the top, cover and steam about 20 minutes.
5. Rest briefly, then cut.

A chopstick that comes out without flour means it is cooked (Nongsaro radish sirutteok). The National Folk Museum's seasonal customs dictionary says to leave the lid off until steam rises, so water does not collect in the cake.

## 4. Why it stays raw

According to the Encyclopedia of Korean Culture, glutinous rice flour packs together and blocks steam; crumble between layers lets steam circulate. So keep layers at 3 cm or less, do not press the flour, and use thinner layers (2–3 cm) for glutinous flour.

Using the cup weights from the dried pumpkin recipe, the Suncheon cake is roughly 1.5 kg glutinous rice to 1 kg dry red beans (our calculation).

## 6. Rites, moving day and the 2026 solstice

The Encyclopedia of Korean Culture traces red bean sirutteok in household rites to the old belief that red wards off misfortune. The seasonal customs dictionary lists it as the cake most often made for the tenth-month rite, for moving house and for receiving the wedding chest. It also records that when the solstice falls early in the eleventh lunar month, families with children made rice cake instead of red bean porridge. The Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute converter gives December 22, 2026 as the 14th of the eleventh lunar month, a mid-month solstice, when either cake or porridge was customary.

## 8. Storage

Rice cake hardens within a day or two as starch retrogrades (RDA, 2010); steaming or microwaving softens it again. Korea's food safety portal gives −18℃ or below for freezing. We found no official freezer life specific to rice cake.

## 9. For growers

A bundle of 1 kg new-crop red beans and 2 kg glutinous rice matches one batch and is easy for buyers to choose. Sort carefully, since crumble shows every damaged bean. Milling flour or selling cooked crumble is processing and may need a business notification; ask the county hygiene office first.
