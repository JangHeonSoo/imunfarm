A rural house in winter runs a wood stove, electric mats and a kerosene heater. There are no sprinklers and neighbors are far away. What wakes a sleeping family is a cheap detector on the ceiling.

> Bottom line: <strong>Under Article 10 of the Act on Installation and Management of Fire-Fighting Systems, every house except apartments and dormitories must have fire extinguishers and stand-alone smoke detectors.</strong> Put one detector on the ceiling of every walled room (and one per 150㎡). Homes and farm sheds with wood stoves, kerosene heaters or gas boilers need a separate carbon monoxide alarm; smoke detectors do not detect CO.

> Earlier records: [camping wood stoves](/en/blog/camping-wood-stove-winter-prep-2026/) and [fan heaters](/en/blog/fan-heater-recommendation-2026/).

## 1. The rule

New houses since 2012 and existing houses since February 2017 must have one extinguisher per household and floor, and a stand-alone detector in each room. Details are set by provincial ordinance. Farm sheds (nongmak) are temporary agricultural buildings, not houses, but any shed used overnight or heated should follow the same standard.

## 2. Where and how

Mount detectors near the center of the ceiling, starting with bedrooms. Use heat-type detectors in kitchens where steam and cooking smoke cause false alarms, and keep detectors away from directly above a wood stove. Press the test button monthly; a short repeated chirp means a low battery. Gyeonggi Province gives battery life as about 10 years.

## 3. Carbon monoxide alarms

Since August 2020, homes installing or replacing a gas boiler must add a CO alarm, and boilers made after August 5, 2020 ship with one. Lodging and glamping sites are also covered. The government briefing advises mounting CO alarms toward the ceiling; follow the product manual for exact height. Put one in the boiler room and in any room or shed where a stove or heater runs. If it sounds, open windows, get out, and call 119 if anyone has a headache or nausea.

## 4. Gas leak alarms

These detect unburned fuel gas. LPG is heavier than air, so mount LPG alarms low; LNG is lighter, so mount those high.

## 5. Free detectors

Fire stations and counties distribute and install detectors and extinguishers free for vulnerable households. Ask the local fire station whether an elderly parent's house qualifies. Otherwise, a battery smoke detector cost about KRW 10,000 on Coupang on November 2, 2026.
