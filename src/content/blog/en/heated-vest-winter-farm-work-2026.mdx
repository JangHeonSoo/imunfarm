On a November morning, hands stay numb in the field an hour after sunrise. A thick down jacket makes bending hard; a thin layer leaves the back cold. Greenhouses are hot inside and cold outside, so clothes come on and off all day. That is why people who work outdoors in winter look for heated vests: a thin vest with heating wires warmed by a USB power bank.

> Bottom line: <strong>When the Korea Consumer Agency tested ten heated vests, the lowest setting averaged 32–47°C and lasted 9–18 hours, while the highest averaged 43–64°C and lasted 4.5–10.5 hours.</strong> Products at the same setting differed widely. Four of the ten exceeded the surface temperature limit (50°C for general clothing, 65°C for clothing worn below freezing) and were voluntarily recalled. For a full day of field work, choose a vest that runs long on low, heats a wide area of the back, and lists temperature, run time, and washing instructions in full.

## 1. How long does the battery last?

| Setting        | Average temperature | Run time       |
| -------------- | ------------------: | -------------- |
| Level 1 (low)  |             32–47°C | 9–18 hours     |
| Level 3 (high) |             43–64°C | 4.5–10.5 hours |

Check which setting an advertised run time refers to. In practice, run high on cold mornings and lower it once you warm up. A spare power bank lets you swap at lunch.

## 2. What to compare

Heating panel location (back only, back and front, neck), the temperature of each setting, an outer shell that wipes clean, whether the battery is included, and complete labels. In the test, nine of ten vests omitted some required label information. Warmth also varied: two products were rated "very good" for heat and insulation.

## 3. How to wear one for farm work

Wear it as a mid layer: over a base layer, under a windbreaker or work jacket. Turn it off inside a warm greenhouse, since sweating with the heat on makes you colder outside. Avoid pressing the heated panel against a seat back for long periods. For heating the shed itself, see the [fan heater guide](/en/blog/fan-heater-recommendation-2026/).

## 4. Low-temperature burns and washing

Low-temperature burns can occur at temperatures that do not feel hot when skin is exposed for a long time. People with reduced sensation, older adults, and children should avoid long use on high, and the vest should be off during sleep. Remove the battery before washing and follow the label. In the test, heating still worked after washing, but some vests transferred color to other clothes. Hand warmers carry the same burn risk when held against skin for long periods.
