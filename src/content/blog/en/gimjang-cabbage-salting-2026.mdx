Every kimjang season, Korean households decide whether to order pre-salted cabbage or buy whole heads and salt them at home. This year the math shifted. Autumn napa cabbage reached the wholesale market two weeks earlier than last year, and its first price is nearly 40% below last November's average. Here is the cost, the salt and the hours, from official sources.

> Bottom line: <strong>In the KAMIS survey on October 28, 2026, a 10kg net of autumn napa cabbage (3 heads, top grade) sold at wholesale in Seoul for KRW 9,400, about KRW 3,100 a head.</strong> That puts 10 heads at about KRW 31,000 and 20 heads at about KRW 63,000 wholesale. Korea Price Research Center budgets 5kg of sea salt for 20 heads. The RDA's Nongsaro guide salts in roughly 15% brine for about 8 hours, turning once or twice. MFDS advises rinsing no more than three times.

Korea Price Research Center priced 5kg of sea salt at KRW 6,000 in traditional markets in 2025. Supermarket pre-orders for 20kg of pre-salted cabbage started at KRW 39,900 for Haenam (29,900 with a card discount), 35,900 for Goesan and 59,900 for Pyeongchang. Salting removes water and trimming removes leaves, so 20kg of pre-salted cabbage takes more than 20kg of raw heads; compare by the head count printed on the box. On ingredients alone, salting at home is cheaper this year, at the cost of a large basin, a night and your back.

## 3. Brine and salt

The RDA's Nongsaro method: halve the heads, dip them in roughly 15% brine, sprinkle coarse salt between the stems, stack them, pour the brine back over, and salt for about 8 hours, turning once or twice. A 15% brine is about 1.8kg of salt in 10 liters of water. The World Institute of Kimchi gives 80–100g of salt per liter as an example and notes that salt, time and temperature vary by region, season and household. Use sun-dried coarse sea salt with the bittern drained.

## 4. Checking, rinsing and draining

Nongsaro describes properly salted cabbage only as "wilted just right"; at home, people bend the thick stem to see that it curves instead of snapping. MFDS tests found that three rinses cut total bacteria by 95% and coliforms by 93%; more rinsing can make the cabbage soft. A Gyeonggi agricultural research center found that three rinses after 6 hours in 10% brine removed aphids and parasite eggs. Drain the halves cut side down on a large rack, and use them the same day or the next morning.

## 5. For growers: harvest and frost

The RDA treats kimjang cabbage as ready 110–120 days after sowing, about 60 days after transplanting, when the head gives 1–2cm when pressed from the top. Seoul's agricultural technology center puts the best harvest time around mid-November. Outer leaves begin to freeze around −3°C (RDA); the Seoul center warns of frost damage at −8°C, or at −3°C after a sudden drop. Do not harvest right after freezing; wait for the plant to recover. Store at 0–3°C and 90–95% humidity. Farms selling pre-salted cabbage should follow the MFDS hygiene guideline and check licensing with the local government first.

## 6. Checklist

- Seoul autumn cabbage KRW 9,400 per 3-head net (Oct 28), 39% below last November's average.
- 10 heads about KRW 31,000 wholesale; 5kg of salt for 20 heads.
- About 15% brine, about 8 hours, turn once or twice.
- Rinse up to three times, drain cut side down, use within a day.
- Growers: harvest before a −3°C forecast.
