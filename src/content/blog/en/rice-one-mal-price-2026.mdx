Families ordering rice cakes for the Lunar New Year and people buying new-crop rice from farms ask the same thing: how many kilograms is a mal, and what does it cost? Mills quote by the mal; stores sell by the kilogram.

> Bottom line: <strong>On October 30, 2026, KAMIS put rice (top grade, 20kg) at KRW 59,500 in Seoul, or KRW 2,975 per kg.</strong> Counting a mal as 8kg, that is about KRW 23,800 at wholesale. New-crop rice was KRW 62,000 per 20kg in Seoul, about KRW 24,800 per mal. Seoul rice is almost unchanged from a year ago (KRW 59,400). The October 15 farm-gate price was KRW 230,320 per 80kg, about KRW 23,030 per 8kg.

> Earlier records: [rice storage containers](/en/blog/rice-storage-container-2026/) and [sesame price per mal](/en/blog/sesame-one-mal-price-2026/).

## 1. How many kg is a mal

A mal is a volume unit of about 18 liters, so there is no official kilogram conversion. In direct sales and at rice-cake mills, 8kg of dry rice is commonly called one mal, but some places use other weights. Always ask for the kilograms.

New-crop rice averaged KRW 59,780 per 20kg across five cities (about KRW 23,900 per mal). Glutinous rice (40kg) was KRW 123,000 in Seoul, 21% below a year ago, or about KRW 24,600 per mal. A 2026 new-crop 10kg bag on Coupang listed at KRW 34,990 on October 31, about KRW 28,000 per mal.

## 3. Will it be cheaper in November?

Seoul rice rose from October to November in six of the last ten years and fell in three (2021, 2023, 2025). There is little reason to wait for a November drop.

## 4. Before making garaetteok

The cost has two parts: the rice and the mill's fee. One mal of your own rice is worth about KRW 23,800–24,800 at wholesale. Mill fees vary by shop and by how close it is to the holiday, so call and ask: how many kg is a mal (dry or soaked), the fee if you bring rice, any extra for slicing, and the pickup date.

## 5. For growers

Write the weight in the listing ("one mal, 8kg"). Use the farm-gate equivalent (about KRW 23,030 per 8kg) as a floor and the wholesale equivalent (about KRW 24,800 for new-crop) as a reference; milling, packing and shipping justify more. Early-variety paddy fell from the mid-KRW 70,000s per 40kg in early September to the high 60,000s by mid-October, according to Nongmin Shinmun.
