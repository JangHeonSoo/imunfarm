Kimchi season fills the freezer with ice packs. Salted seafood, oysters and pork for bossam all arrive with two or three packs per box. When you finally clear them out, the question is whether to cut them open, recycle them as vinyl, or bin them. This post fills the gap we left in our [styrofoam recycling guide](/blog/styrofoam-recycling-kimjang-box-2026/).

> Bottom line: <strong>Keep gel ice packs sealed and put them in the pay-as-you-throw general waste bag.</strong> If your neighborhood has an ice pack collection bin, take clean ones there. Cut water packs open, pour out the water, rinse the film and recycle it as vinyl. Never pour gel down the sink or toilet: it is microplastic and ends up in the sea (Korea Policy Briefing, Seoul Metropolitan Government).

## 1. Gel or water?

Gel packs contain superabsorbent polymer (SAP), tiny plastic beads that soak up water and set like jelly. Eco packs hold water, sometimes with starch and salt. Check the film first: "water", "eco" or a dotted cutting line usually means a water pack. We found no official guide for telling them apart by touch, so if in doubt, treat it as gel and bin it sealed. Gel packs fell from about 71% of the market in 2019 to 49% in 2020, according to the government.

## 2. Where each type goes

| Type                   | Where it goes                                          | Do not                         |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Gel (SAP) pack         | Sealed, into the general waste bag or a collection bin | Squeeze gel into drains        |
| Water pack             | Cut open, pour out water, rinse film, recycle as vinyl | Put it in vinyl recycling full |
| Torn or dirty gel pack | General waste bag                                      | Put it in a collection bin     |

## 3. Collection bins

Some districts collect clean gel packs, wash them and give them to market traders. Dongjak-gu in Seoul swaps five gel packs for one 10L waste bag every Thursday at community centers (since April 2025). Paju runs bins at 15 community centers and 66 apartment complexes from April 1 to November 30, 2026. Gangseo-gu accepts only clean, intact gel packs. Bupyeong-gu in Incheon ended its program on December 1, 2025 after collections fell from 140,376 packs in 2021 to 18,020 in 2025. Ask your community center whether a bin exists near you.

## 4. Reuse and farm shipping

Seoul encourages reusing clean ice packs, and the government article says refreezing causes no problem. Rinse and dry them, and discard torn or swollen ones. Farms shipping side dishes, rice cakes, meat or fish directly to customers can switch to water packs, which customers can dispose of easily. Since 2023, gel packs have carried a waste levy of KRW 313 per kg (about KRW 94 for a 300g pack), paid by manufacturers and importers; packs that are collected and reused are exempt. Pack weight counts toward parcel fees, so use only as many as you need.

## 5. Conclusion

Check what is inside. Gel stays sealed and goes in the waste bag or a bin; water gets cut, emptied and the film recycled. Keep a few clean packs for your own parcels.
