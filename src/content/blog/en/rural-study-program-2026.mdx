Seoul now sends more than 1,000 students a year to small rural schools for six months or a year. Participation in the Seoul Metropolitan Office of Education's rural study program rose from 228 students in 2021 to 1,127 in 2026.

> Bottom line: <strong>Seoul public elementary (grades 1–6) and middle school (grades 1–2) students transfer for 6 or 12 months to rural schools in South Jeolla, North Jeolla, Gangwon, Jeju, Incheon's islands, and from spring 2027 South Gyeongsang.</strong> Last year's spring intake took applications November 26 to December 1 through the student's current school. Seoul's support was up to KRW 600,000 per month (2026 fall intake reports). As of November 10, the 2027 spring notice had not yet been confirmed.

## 1. Three types

Family-stay (the family moves together), homestay (the student lives with a farm family) and center (the student lives at a center with staff). In fall 2026, 536 of 569 students were family-stay and 33 center-based.

## 2. Schedule

Media reported the 2027 spring intake for November. On October 14, 2026, Seoul's education office announced an agreement adding South Gyeongsang. Last year: video briefing Nov 24, applications Nov 26–Dec 1, provisional placement Dec 8, site visits Dec 9–15, final application Dec 16–18, confirmation Dec 23.

## 3. Support

Seoul: up to KRW 600,000 a month, for up to one year (2026 fall reports). North Jeolla: KRW 300,000–500,000 a month by number of children (2025). Incheon: 600,000 per student, plus 200,000 per student for two or more (2024). South Chungcheong: about 600,000 per household in stay costs (October 2026 report). South Jeolla and Gangwon amounts and out-of-pocket costs for center and homestay types could not be confirmed. Check the local notice for the school you choose.

## 4. Do families stay?

In fall 2026, 521 of 569 (92%) were extensions. A Seoul council audit reported extension rates rising from 70% (2021) to 93% (2025) and satisfaction of 88% (October 2025). By region: Gangwon 231, South Jeolla 124, North Jeolla 109, Jeju 105.

## 5. Try a short stay first

Rural experience villages are designated under the Urban-Rural Exchange Promotion Act (Art. 5). There were 1,195 nationwide at the end of 2023, with about 11.25 million visitors that year. A weekend there shows school distance, winter cold and how far the shops are.
