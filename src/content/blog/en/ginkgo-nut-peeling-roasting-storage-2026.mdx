Streets covered in yellow ginkgo leaves come with a smell. The day you pick up fallen ginkgo fruit, or a neighbor hands you a bag, the questions begin. The soft pulp stinks and itches on bare skin, the shell is hard and the inner skin clings. With the right order it is simpler than it looks.

> Bottom line: <strong>remove the pulp with gloves, crack the hard shell with a cracker or a pan, and always eat them cooked and in small amounts.</strong> Korea's Ministry of Food and Drug Safety advised up to 10 nuts a day for adults and 2–3 for children (2014 report). On November 4, 2026, domestic shelled ginkgo cost KRW 16,000–22,000 per kg on Coupang, and 2026 in-shell ginkgo KRW 6,000–10,000 per kg.

## 1. Removing the pulp

The edible part is the kernel inside a hard white shell, wrapped in soft yellow pulp. The smell comes from the pulp.

1. **Wear rubber or plastic gloves.** Contact with the pulp can cause severe skin inflammation (MSD Manual).
2. **Soak in water for a few days** until the pulp softens. Do it outdoors.
3. **Rub off the pulp and rinse several times** until only white shells remain.
4. **Dry thoroughly in the shade.** Damp nuts mold. This dried stage is what is sold as in-shell ginkgo.

## 2. Cracking the shell

- **Ginkgo cracker.** Press just enough to crack the shell, not crush the kernel.
- **Pan.** Roll the nuts in a dry or salted pan until the shells split.
- **Microwave.** Put in-shell nuts in a folded paper bag and stop once they start popping.

Rub off the thin brown inner skin with kitchen paper while still warm.

## 3. Roasting

Always cook ginkgo; both the food safety ministry and Seoul city say so. Roll shelled nuts in a dry or lightly salted pan over low heat until they turn translucent green, or microwave them briefly, or cook them with rice or porridge.

## 4. How many per day

Ginkgo contains a toxic compound, methylpyridoxine. The Ministry of Food and Drug Safety advised in 2014 that adults eat up to 10 and children 2–3 a day, cooked only. Seoul city gave the same guidance. This post does not discuss health benefits.

## 5. Storage

We found no official storage periods, so we give principles only. Keep dry in-shell nuts cool and ventilated or in the fridge. Freeze shelled nuts in single servings of about ten, which also keeps you within the daily amount. Do not refreeze.

## 7. Street trees

Seoul harvests fruit from 25,127 fruit-bearing street ginkgo trees (24.4% of 102,794) from the third week of September to before the end of October, tests it for heavy metals and pesticide residue, and donates what passes to senior centers and welfare facilities (2024). We could not confirm the legal rules on picking up street ginkgo, so we do not state them. Do not knock nuts off trees.

## 8. For growers

In-shell ginkgo sells for KRW 6,000–10,000 per kg online, shelled frozen ginkgo for two to three times that, and 250g packs give the highest per-kg price. Shelling adds labor, freezing and shipping costs. Wear gloves and long sleeves when handling pulp.
