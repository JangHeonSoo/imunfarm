Once you put out a winter shelter and start feeding a stray cat, the next spring brings a new problem. You hear kittens behind the shed, and one cat becomes three or four. Near a farm shed or a cattle barn it happens even faster. That is when people look for TNR: trap, neuter, return. In Korea it runs as a local government program.

> Bottom line: <strong>Apply for stray cat neutering at your city, county or district office, through the animal protection team.</strong> Some Seoul districts also take requests through the 120 Dasan call center. A vet performs the surgery and removes about 1cm from the tip of the left ear to mark the cat. Cats under 2kg and pregnant or nursing cats are released without surgery (Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs cat neutering rules). We found no official wording on what the applicant pays, so ask your district office when you apply.

Related: how to choose or build a winter shelter is covered in [Winter Shelters for Stray Cats 2026](/blog/stray-cat-winter-shelter-winter-prep-2026/).

## 1. Where to apply

The rule is the ministry notice "Cat Neutering Program Guidelines" (No. 2021-88, amended November 30, 2021, effective January 1, 2022). It applies only to programs run or commissioned by local governments, so the application goes to your local office.

The Seoul Metropolitan Government's page (updated March 26, 2026) tells residents to apply to their district office. Examples from 2026 notices:

| Area             | How to apply                                                                                          | Team                   |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Geumcheon, Seoul | Phone the district office or the 120 call center (24 hours)                                           | Animal protection      |
| Gangdong, Seoul  | Online civil petition on the district website, all year except extreme cold and heat                  | Animal welfare team    |
| Yeonsu, Incheon  | Visit with ID or apply online; the applicant traps, takes the cat to a designated vet and releases it | Animal protection team |

In rural counties, the livestock or animal protection team usually handles it. The national animal protection system (animal.go.kr) lists the responsible team for each local government.

## 2. Who pays

None of the official notices we checked said "free for applicants" or named an applicant fee. The program is funded from local budgets. Gangdong set aside KRW 100 million of its KRW 136 million animal welfare budget for TNR in 2026. Nationally, a report citing the 2025 animal welfare survey (Dailyvet, July 9, 2026) put the 2025 total at 128,514 cats and about KRW 25.19 billion, roughly KRW 196,000 per cat.

## 3. What a tipped ear means

A cat with the tip of its left ear cut straight has already been neutered. The rules require removing about 1cm from the left ear tip so the cat can be recognized from a distance and is not trapped twice. If an ear-tipped cat is caught again, it is released without surgery.

## 4. Trap, surgery, return

- **Trapping.** Use a safe box trap with a sign giving its purpose and a contact number. Cover the trap with a cloth once a cat is inside.
- **Released at once.** Cats under 2kg, pregnant or nursing cats, and ear-tipped cats.
- **Surgery.** A vet operates within 24 hours of capture. If nursing is confirmed under anesthesia, there is no surgery and the cat is released when it wakes.
- **Release.** Males at least 24 hours after surgery, females at least 72 hours. Cats go back to where they were caught.

In severe cold, release is postponed if temperatures are forecast to stay at or below 0°C for three or more days. The rules set no banned months, but local governments set breaks. Yeonsu pauses in July and August and from December to mid-February. A draft amendment allowing surgery on cats under 2kg at a vet's discretion went out for comment in April 2026. As of November 28, the national law database still lists No. 2021-88 as current.

## 5. Cats around a farm shed: when to apply

- **Before winter.** Many local programs pause or delay releases from about December to February. Miss November and you wait until spring.
- **Spring is kitten season.** Pregnant and nursing cats are released without surgery, so many trapped cats cannot be treated.
- **Weekend farm sheds.** A female returns 72 hours after surgery. Agree on a date when someone will be at the shed, and keep the shelter entrance open.

## 6. Conclusion

After the shelter and the feeding spot, neutering is the next step. Ask your local office or 120 first and apply before the winter pause. A cat with a tipped left ear is already done, so count only the rest.
