How much is ginger per kilogram if you buy a 10 kg box? In November 2026 the answer depends on the city. Seoul eased slightly over the month, while Busan and Daejeon jumped.

> Bottom line: <strong>On November 20, 2026, KAMIS recorded top-grade domestic ginger at 58,300 won per 10 kg wholesale in Seoul, or 5,830 won per kg.</strong> Seoul is 5.4% lower than a month earlier (61,600 won) and 3.0% higher than a year ago (56,600 won). The five-city average was 65,500 won, up 18.4% on the month and 18.0% on the year. Imported ginger (mid grade) was 80,600 won in Seoul, more expensive than top-grade domestic ginger.

## 2. Why is imported ginger dearer?

Imported mid-grade ginger has stayed above domestic top-grade ginger all year (80,000 vs 56,600 won a year ago; 80,600 vs 58,300 won now). KAMIS data does not explain why, and we found no official explanation. For shoppers the takeaway is simple: at wholesale, domestic ginger is currently cheaper, so check the origin label rather than assuming imports are the budget option.

## 3. Splitting a 10 kg box at home

Rural Development Administration guidance (Nongsaro) puts ideal storage at 12–14°C and 85–90%+ humidity. Below 10°C ginger suffers chilling injury; above 18°C it sprouts. A home fridge is too cold for long storage.

- Peel, mince or slice 2–3 kg and freeze flat.
- Turn 3–4 kg into ginger syrup the week it arrives (equal weights of ginger and sugar is common).
- Keep the rest unwashed, wrapped in newspaper, in a cool spot that does not freeze.

## 4. For growers: when to sell stored ginger

Seoul top-grade monthly averages last season: 54,815 won (Nov 2025), 60,386 won (Dec 2025), 60,000 won (Jan–Apr 2026), 61,600 won (Jul–Oct 2026). Holding until December paid about 10%; holding into spring added little. This year, regional markets already rose sharply in November. The top/mid grade gap in Seoul is 9,000 won per 10 kg, which rewards careful sorting. If storing, cure at 25°C and 95% humidity for three days, then hold at 12–14°C, and sell any softening lots immediately.
