Direct-sale sesame in Korea is still priced by the mal, an old volume unit. Buyers ask "how much for a mal this year," but a mal can mean 5kg or 6kg depending on the seller. The quickest check is to convert the wholesale price to a kilogram and multiply back.

> Bottom line: <strong>In the KAMIS survey on October 29, 2026, domestic white sesame (top grade, 30kg) averaged KRW 703,000 across five cities, about KRW 23,430 per kg.</strong> Seoul was KRW 773,000 (about KRW 25,770/kg). A 6kg mal works out to about KRW 141,000 at wholesale (Seoul: about KRW 155,000); a 5kg mal to about KRW 117,000. Prices are 6.8% below early October and 11.8% below a year ago.

> Earlier records: [domestic sesame prices 2026](/en/blog/domestic-sesame-price-2026/), [domestic sesame oil prices](/en/blog/domestic-sesame-oil-price-2026/) and [perilla seed prices in late October](/en/blog/perilla-seed-price-late-october-2026/).

## 1. How many kg is a mal

A mal is a volume unit of about 18 liters (ten doe). Its weight depends on how dry and clean the seed is, so sellers now state kilograms. A Gangwon regional farm mall listed "new-crop sesame 6kg, one mal" at KRW 210,000 (list KRW 240,000) on October 30, which is KRW 35,000/kg, about 36% above the Seoul wholesale equivalent. Some sellers use 5kg.

Imported sesame (medium grade, 30kg) averaged KRW 213,600 for Chinese (about KRW 7,120/kg, 30% of domestic) and KRW 157,000 for Indian (about KRW 5,230/kg, four cities). Daily prices are on the [domestic sesame price page](/en/prices/sesame/).

## 3. October trend and the November question

The five-city average fell from KRW 754,000 on October 1 to KRW 731,400 on October 13 and KRW 703,000 on October 29. Seoul held at KRW 773,000 while Gwangju, Busan, Daegu and Daejeon came down as new-crop sesame arrived. In Seoul's KAMIS monthly averages from 2015 to 2025, the October-to-November change was within 1% in 7 of 11 years; the big moves (2020: +11.5%, 2021: +14.8%) were increases. Waiting for November has rarely meant a lower price.

## 4. How much oil from a mal

No official standard yield exists. Research on Korean sesame germplasm put crude fat at 46.5–50.9%. At 48% oil and a density of 0.92, a 6kg mal holds at most about 3.1 liters of oil, or about 10 bottles of 300ml; a 5kg mal about 8.7 bottles. Real pressing yields less, so ask the mill how many ml it usually gets per kg.

## 5. For growers

State the weight in the listing ("one mal, 6kg"), treat the wholesale equivalent as a floor for direct sales that include cleaning, packing and shipping, state the crop year, and do not count on a November jump.

## 6. Red bean per mal

Domestic red beans (top grade, 40kg) averaged KRW 644,200 on October 29, about KRW 16,100/kg, 16.6% below a year ago. Ask how many kg the mal is and multiply. See the [red bean price page](/en/prices/red-bean/).
