Yuzu groves on Korea's south coast turn yellow in mid-November. Fresh yuzu is in the shops only briefly and does not keep long, so most people turn it into syrup (yuja-cheong) the day it arrives.

> Bottom line: <strong>The most common method is yuzu and sugar at 1:1 by weight.</strong> We found no official ratio from the RDA or the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety. Yuzu is harvested in November, and Goheung grows about 60% of the national crop (YTN, 2023). On November 15, 2026, Goheung new-crop yuzu sold for about 8,800–11,200 won per kg on Coupang. The 2025 Goheung purchase price for top grade was 7,000–7,500 won per kg.

> Earlier notes: ginger syrup made the same way is in [Ginger Syrup 2026](/en/blog/ginger-syrup-homemade-cost-2026/).

## 1. Season and regions

The RDA webzine describes yuzu as a fruit "harvested in November". Main regions are Goheung, Wando and Yeosu in Jeollanam-do, and Geoje, Tongyeong and Namhae in Gyeongsangnam-do. Goheung produced 8,770 tonnes from 643 ha in 2025 (Herald Economy).

## 2. Choosing, washing and seeding

Pick heavy, glossy, fragrant fruit. Cosmetic blemishes do not matter for syrup. The RDA webzine says fresh yuzu wrapped in newspaper keeps about 7 days at 0–5℃. Rinse quickly under running water (long soaking loses water-soluble vitamins), dry completely, halve, remove every seed (seeds make it bitter), and slice thinly.

## 3. Sugar and jars

| Yuzu (seeded) | Sugar at 1:1 | Less sweet (example) |
| ------------: | -----------: | -------------------: |
|         500 g |        500 g |                400 g |
|          1 kg |         1 kg |                800 g |

Less sugar means faster spoilage, so refrigerate and eat it sooner. Use glass, not metal; metal speeds vitamin C oxidation. Sterilise jars with boiling water and dry them. Leave the jar a day or two at room temperature until the sugar dissolves, then refrigerate. No official ripening time exists; most people start after 1–2 weeks.

## 4. Prices

KAMIS does not survey yuzu. Coupang listings on November 15, 2026: 3 kg "ugly" (ungraded) 29,500 won, 5 kg large 56,000 won, 10 kg medium 88,000 won.

| 2025 Goheung grade | Purchase price per kg |
| ------------------ | --------------------: |
| Top                |       7,000–7,500 won |
| Medium             |       6,000–6,500 won |
| Low                |       4,000–4,500 won |
| Culls              |        from 2,000 won |

Prices ran 20–40% above expectations in 2025 because summer heat reduced flower buds. No 2026 purchase price had been published as of November 15.

## 5. Yuzu tea

Stir one or two spoons (20–30 g) of syrup into hot water. The RDA webzine says yuzu has three times the vitamin C of lemon; the NHIS webzine gives 95–140 mg per 100 g.
