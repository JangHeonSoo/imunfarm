Red scallops (hong-garibi) fill Korean fish markets from late autumn. This year Seoul wholesale prices look 40% higher than last year, yet Busan is lower. Which number should you trust?

> Bottom line: <strong>On November 16, 2026, KAMIS red scallop wholesale prices ranged from 3,160 won per kg in Busan to 7,120 won in Daejeon.</strong> Seoul's 7,000 won is 40% above last November, but the five-city average of 5,788 won is close to a year earlier (5,996 won).

## Prices by city

| City    | Nov 16, 2026 | Nov 17, 2025 |
| ------- | -----------: | -----------: |
| Seoul   |    7,000 won |    5,000 won |
| Busan   |    3,160 won |    4,250 won |
| Daegu   |    5,830 won |    5,900 won |
| Gwangju |    5,830 won |    5,830 won |
| Daejeon |    7,120 won |    9,000 won |
| Average |    5,788 won |    5,996 won |

Seoul's price has not changed in 19 survey days since October 21, and last season it sat at 5,000 won for months. Daily prices in other cities show the market better. See [red scallop prices](/prices/scallop-red/) and [oyster prices](/prices/oyster/).

## When is it cheapest?

KAMIS surveys run from October to April or May. Last season (2025–2026), Busan, Daegu and Gwangju prices peaked in November and fell to 35–65% of that level by March to May. The season before, Busan and Gwangju prices rose slightly into February and March. One season is not enough to promise a spring discount.

## Online prices (November 17, before account coupons)

A 2 kg chilled pack was 13,490 won (6,745 won per kg). Washed 5 kg boxes from Goseong and Tongyeong were 17,900 to 26,700 won (3,580 to 5,340 won per kg).

## Farm losses

Gyeongnam produces about 95% of Korea's scallops. Goseong's Jaran Bay reported low-oxygen damage in 2025, and in 2026 low oxygen, freshwater inflow and high water temperatures hit 53 farms (84 ha), according to local broadcaster KNN. No official statement links these losses to current prices.
