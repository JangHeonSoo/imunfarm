A ton bag of rice weighs 500kg. No one moves that by hand, which is why small forklifts sit in farm warehouses and co-op depots across Korea. The common mistake is assuming a small forklift needs no license.

> Bottom line: <strong>forklifts under 3 tons still require a construction machinery operator license, but you can get it without any exam — complete 12 hours of designated training (6 hours theory, 6 hours practice) and apply at your city or county office.</strong> You must already hold a Class 1 ordinary driver license. Tuition varies by institution; a 2026 Sokcho city program listed it at KRW 300,000. Forklifts of 3 tons and over require passing the national craftsman exam instead.

## 1. The legal basis

Article 26(1) of the Construction Machinery Management Act requires an operator license from the mayor or county chief for any construction machinery. The default route is the national technical qualification, but Article 26(4) lets "small construction machinery" operators substitute a designated training course for the exam. The Enforcement Rule, Article 73(2), lists "forklifts under 3 tons" as small construction machinery, and Article 73(3) requires holders to have an appropriate driver license under the Road Traffic Act rules — in practice, Class 1 ordinary. Operating without the license is punishable by imprisonment or fines under occupational safety rules, as a 2026 municipal notice warns.

## 2. What the 12 hours cover

Based on the 2026 farmer course run by the Jeonbuk agricultural technology institute: machinery engines and electrics (2h), hydraulics (2h), construction machinery law and road traffic law (2h), then 6 hours of driving practice. After the course you take the completion certificate to the city or county office and receive the operator license.

## 3. Cost

There is no fixed national fee. In the 2026 Sokcho program, tuition was KRW 300,000, with the city and chamber of commerce covering most of it. Farmers should first check their provincial agricultural technology institute or local extension center: these run dedicated small-machinery license courses for farmers, sometimes free or subsidized — Jeonbuk's 2026 course targeted farmers holding a Class 1 license, 12 hours per cohort.

## 4. How it differs from the craftsman certificate

The under-3-ton route is training only and limits you to forklifts under 3 tons. The forklift craftsman qualification (jigecha unjeon gineungsa) is a national exam — written CBT plus a practical driving test, administered by HRDK via Q-net — with no tonnage limit, and is the better path for employment. Exam fees and schedules are listed on Q-net.

## 5. Electric forklifts and hand pallet trucks

Per the guidance attached to the Sokcho notice: a battery-powered forklift with solid tires used only inside the workplace does not count as construction machinery, so the training completion certificate alone suffices indoors. Diesel forklifts, air-filled tires, or any road driving require the full license. A manual hand pallet truck has no drive motor and falls outside the forklift license system entirely — often the simpler answer when all you need is moving pallets across a flat warehouse floor.
