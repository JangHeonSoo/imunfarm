Kimchi recipes say "two cups of chili powder," but chili powder is sold by the kilogram. Perilla seed is sold by the "mal," which some sellers mean as 5 kg and others as 6 kg. Ginger syrup calls for ginger and sugar at one to one. Guessing each time leaves seasoning too salty or left over. A kitchen scale turns these questions into numbers.

> Bottom line: <strong>For kimjang seasoning and grain bought by the kilogram, a digital scale with a 5 kg maximum and 1 g steps is enough.</strong> 0.1 g steps matter only for baking or tiny amounts. The tare button, which zeroes out the bowl, is the feature you will use most. A home scale, however, must not be used to weigh produce for sale: under Korea's Measures Act, scales used in trade must pass a periodic inspection every two years.

## 1. How much capacity?

| What you weigh                        | Rough weight                              | Scale             |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Chili powder for 20 cabbages          | 3 kg (Korea Price Research Center survey) | 5 kg max          |
| One "mal" of perilla or sesame        | 5–6 kg (varies by seller)                 | 5 kg, in two lots |
| 1 kg portions of chili powder or seed | 1 kg                                      | 3 kg or more      |
| Minced garlic, salt                   | tens of grams                             | 1 g steps         |
| Yeast, herbs                          | a few grams                               | 0.1 g steps       |

A large stainless bowl alone can weigh several hundred grams, so a 3 kg scale fills up quickly. 5 kg is comfortable for kimjang.

## 2. Tare, batteries, and water resistance

Look for a large, easy tare button (you will press it with seasoned hands), a wide platform that does not hide the display under a big bowl, and an auto-off delay long enough for slow additions. Battery-powered scales suit households that use them a few times a year. Check what "waterproof" means on each product: washable, or just splash-resistant.

## 3. Weighing kimjang seasoning

A 2025 survey for a four-person household counted 3 kg of chili powder, 2.4 kg of peeled garlic, and 800 g of ginger for 20 cabbages, about 150 g of chili powder per cabbage. Write down what you actually used this year in grams, and next year's shopping list is done. See also the [jjokpa price post](/en/blog/scallion-price-gimjang-2026/) and the [chili powder price post](/en/blog/gochugaru-price-2026/).

## 4. Can farmers use a kitchen scale for direct sales?

No. According to the Korean Agency for Technology and Standards, non-automatic scales under 10 tonnes used in trade, including electronic indicating scales, are inspected by provincial governments every two years. Local inspection notices exclude scales marked for home or education use that lack type approval, which also means such scales are not meant for trade. Using an uninspected scale in trade can bring a fine. Use a type-approved trade scale for packing produce for sale, and keep the kitchen scale for recipes. Korea's produce standards define the trading unit as the net weight excluding packaging, so tare the bag first.
