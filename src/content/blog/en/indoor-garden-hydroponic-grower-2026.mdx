Garden lettuce ends in November. A countertop hydroponic grower with an LED bar keeps salad greens coming through winter, and the first question is the power bill.

> Bottom line: <strong>Under KEPCO residential low-voltage rates, a 25 W LED grower run 16 hours a day uses 12 kWh a month and adds about KRW 1,800 (tier 1), KRW 3,000 (tier 2) or KRW 4,200 (tier 3), including climate and fuel adjustment charges and VAT, excluding the power industry fund.</strong> Lettuce grows best at 15–20°C, and Nongsaro gives EC 1.5–2.0 dS/m and pH 5.5–6.5 for leafy greens.

## 1. Rates

KEPCO's residential low-voltage rates have not changed since November 9, 2023. Outside summer: up to 200 kWh, KRW 910 base and KRW 120.0 per kWh; 201–400 kWh, KRW 1,600 and KRW 214.6; above 400 kWh, KRW 7,300 and KRW 307.3. The fuel cost adjustment for Q4 2026 stays at +KRW 5 per kWh and the climate charge is KRW 9.0. With 10% VAT, each extra kWh costs about KRW 147, 252 or 353 depending on the household's tier.

## 2. Monthly cost (16 hours a day, 30 days)

| LED power | kWh/month |          Tier 1 |          Tier 2 |          Tier 3 |
| --------- | --------: | --------------: | --------------: | --------------: |
| 15 W      |       7.2 | about KRW 1,060 | about KRW 1,810 | about KRW 2,540 |
| 25 W      |      12.0 | about KRW 1,770 | about KRW 3,020 | about KRW 4,240 |
| 40 W      |      19.2 | about KRW 2,830 | about KRW 4,830 | about KRW 6,790 |

Households near the 200 kWh boundary pay a little more because the base charge steps up. For comparison, red leaf lettuce (grade A, 4 kg) averaged KRW 17,040 at wholesale on October 8, 2026, about KRW 4,260 per kilogram. The power bill is small, but with the machine and nutrients the real value is fresh greens in winter rather than savings.

## 3. Growing conditions

| Item            | Guide                          | Source                 |
| --------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| Temperature     | 15–20°C; bolting above 25°C    | MAFRA                  |
| Nutrient EC     | 1.5–2.0 dS/m for leafy greens  | Nongsaro               |
| pH              | 5.5–6.5                        | Nongsaro               |
| Germination     | 7–10 days                      | NIHHS (garden lettuce) |
| First harvest   | 25–30 days after transplanting | NIHHS (garden lettuce) |
| Winter seedling | about 35 days                  | NIHHS (garden lettuce) |

RDA warns that reusing nutrient solution for a long time while only matching EC throws the ion balance off; replace it on the schedule the product recommends.

## 4. What to check before buying

LED wattage (ask the seller if it is not listed), a built-in timer, adjustable light height, tank size and the price of replacement pods. Unheated balconies get too cold at night; a living room works better.
