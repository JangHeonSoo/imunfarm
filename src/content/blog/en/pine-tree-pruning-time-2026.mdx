Once the persimmons are picked and the leaves drop, every overgrown branch in a country yard is visible, and the question is whether to cut now.

> Bottom line: <strong>the Korea Forest Service landscape tree guide says to prune conifers in early spring, keep autumn (September–November) work light, and cut heavy limbs before bud break.</strong> Pinch pine candles by 1/3–2/3 when they reach 5–10cm. RDA Nongsaro puts persimmon winter pruning at mid-February to late March. If the job needs the top of a ladder, hire it out.

## 1. Pine pruning calendar

From the Korea Forest Service guide: conifers are pruned in early spring; heavy limbs before bud break (cutting them in late spring weakens the tree); long branches from April until the monsoon; no hard pruning in summer; only light trimming in autumn. In November, remove dead, broken or roof-touching branches and leave shaping for early spring.

## 2. Candle pinching

When new pine shoots reach 5–10cm, pinching off 1/3–2/3 makes new buds form at the cut and shortens internodes. Do it before the shoots turn woody. The guide gives a shoot length, not a calendar date.

## 3. Persimmon winter pruning

Nongsaro's sweet persimmon video: mid-February to late March is best; large orchards may start in December or early January; in frost-prone sites prune less or later; cut diseased wood 2–3cm beyond the lesion. The Nongsaro work schedule adds: space side branches about 50cm apart and lower tree height gradually over 3–4 years.

## 4. Heavy limbs: three cuts

Undercut 10–15cm out to about a third of the diameter, cut from above slightly further out to drop the limb, then make the final cut where the branch collar ends, angled slightly outward. Brush off sawdust and apply wound dressing.

## 5. Ladder safety

A news report on the RDA's 2025 farmer injury survey said ladders account for 46.8% of farm-tool accidents. KOSHA GUIDE G-112-2014: limit ladder work to 30 minutes, keep three points of contact, never straighten an A-frame into a single ladder, keep your center of gravity inside the rails, keep people clear of falling branches. The Ministry of Employment and Labor's guidance allows work only on A-frame ladders up to 3.5m and not on the top step or the one below it.
