Boiling soybeans takes a day. The hard part is the month after, when the blocks dry and ferment. If the room is too warm or too damp, the wrong mold settles on the surface.

> Bottom line: <strong>Soak the soybeans in cold water for more than 10 hours and boil them for more than 2 hours until soft. Shape 1–2 kg blocks, dry them for 1–2 weeks, then ferment them for 2–3 weeks at 30°C or below and 60% humidity or below.</strong> White mold is a sign the meju is fermenting well. On November 23, 2026, KAMIS domestic white soybeans (top grade, 40 kg) averaged 225,200 won across five cities, about 5,630 won per kg.

Earlier records: [Meju soybean prices, November 2026](/blog/meju-soybean-price-november-2026/) and [How to make cheonggukjang 2026](/blog/cheonggukjang-how-to-make-2026/).

## When to make meju

Meju is made after the weather turns cold, from around Ipdong (early November) through December. The Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS) recommends fermenting meju at 30°C or below with humidity at 60% or below. Warm late-autumn days make it easy for unwanted microbes to spread before the inside of the block dries.

Fermentation takes almost a month, so meju made in late November is ready by late December. Many households then make soy sauce and doenjang in January or February.

## Steps

| Step    | What to do                              | Guide                           | Source                |
| ------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Soak    | Soak washed soybeans in cold water      | 10+ hours                       | Sunchang guide (2026) |
| Boil    | Boil until soft enough to crush by hand | 2+ hours                        | Sunchang guide (2026) |
| Shape   | Drain, mash and shape square blocks     | 1–2 kg per block                | MFDS (2021)           |
| Dry     | Dry in a well-ventilated place          | 1–2 weeks                       | Sunchang guide (2026) |
| Ferment | Keep in a warm place                    | 2–3 weeks, ≤30°C, ≤60% humidity | Sunchang guide, MFDS  |

Block size varies by source. MFDS suggests 1–2 kg, while an RDA booklet describes smaller 500 g–1 kg blocks. Smaller blocks dry through more easily.

## Mold and straw

The Rural Development Administration (RDA) calls the white mold on meju "meju flowers," a sign of good fermentation. Meju fermented with koji mold is covered in yellow fuzz. We could not find an official rule for discarding meju by mold color.

MFDS warns that aflatoxin, a mold toxin, does not break down easily when heated. Keep the temperature and humidity in range, and before making soy sauce, brush off the surface mold, wash the blocks and dry them in the sun.

Rice straw is traditional, but a 2010 RDA study found no clear difference in microbes between meju made with and without straw. Straw is mainly useful for hanging blocks so air flows around them.

## For growers

Households making meju look for top-grade beans. In Seoul on November 23, top grade sold for 227,000 won per 40 kg and medium grade for 200,000 won, a gap of 27,000 won. Sorting out broken and insect-damaged beans pays. Sell in 5 kg and 10 kg units before the meju season peaks. Anyone planning to sell finished meju should check food business reporting rules with the local government first.
