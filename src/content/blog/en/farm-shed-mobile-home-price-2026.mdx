Weekend farmers in Korea eventually ask the same question: why not drop a small prefab cabin on the field? Ads promise a "6-pyeong mobile home" for under KRW 10 million. But the same box can be a farm hut, a rural stay shelter or a house, and each comes with different rules on size, sleeping and permits.

> Bottom line: <strong>a farm hut (nongmak) must be 20 m² or less and cannot be used as a dwelling.</strong> To stay overnight, register it as a rural stay shelter (33 m² or less), which needs road access for fire trucks and residential fire equipment. Living in it as a house requires converting the farmland and a building permit or report. As of October 6, 2026, 6-pyeong units on Coupang ranged from about KRW 9.4 million for a steel container hut to about KRW 23.4 million for a modular unit, with transport, foundation, power, water and septic usually extra.

## 1. Three categories.

| Item     | Farm hut                  | Rural stay shelter                     | House                                 |
| -------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Size     | 20 m² or less             | 33 m² or less                          | Depends on land use                   |
| Sleeping | Not as a dwelling         | Temporary lodging for farm work        | Yes                                   |
| Filing   | Temporary building report | Temporary building report              | Farmland conversion + building permit |
| Road     | No rule                   | Must face a road usable by fire trucks | Building Act road rules               |

The rules come from Articles 3-2 and 3-4 of the Enforcement Rule of the Farmland Act, amended September 22, 2026. Six pyeong is about 19.8 m², just under the hut limit. Ten pyeong is about 33.06 m², slightly over the shelter limit.

## 2. Farm hut requirements.

Not for residential use; 20 m² or less in floor area (and in building area if eaves project 1 m or more); parking and deck within the ministry's notified limits; a temporary building report; registration in the farmland ledger; and local building ordinance standards. A separate farm-work toilet may not be installed on a parcel that already has a farm hut or shelter.

## 3. Rural stay shelter requirements.

33 m² or less (combined with any farm hut on the same parcel); not in disaster-prevention or collapse-risk zones; facing a road usable by fire trucks; a parcel at least twice the combined area of the shelter, deck, parking and external septic tank; a temporary building report and ledger registration; and residential fire equipment. Local ordinances may allow three or more extensions; the ministry said shelters could be used for up to 12 years.

Ask separately about transport, crane, foundation, power hookup, water, septic, filing paperwork and decks. Power and water vary the most from site to site. For off-grid power, see our [farm shed solar cost guide](/en/blog/farm-shed-small-solar-off-grid-cost/), and for winter, our [frozen pipe prevention guide](/en/blog/farm-shed-winter-frozen-pipe-prevention/).

## 5. Conclusion.

Decide first whether the box will be a farm hut, a shelter or a house; that sets the size limit and the paperwork. File before you install, and check road, power and water before buying land.
