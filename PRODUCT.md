# IMUN.FARM Product

Korean farm-economics blog (imun.farm) with English mirror. Readers are working farmers, returning-to-farm households and hobby growers in Korea, mostly arriving from Naver and Google search on a phone.

## Surfaces

- Blog posts (Read): price outlooks, cost guides, equipment reviews. KO is primary.
- 시세 /prices/ (Operate): daily KAMIS wholesale prices, movers, charts.
- 계산기 /tools/ (Operate): market proceeds, solar income, cold-storage cost.
- 지원 /support/ (Read + light Operate): farm subsidy programs, what you get, who qualifies, when and where to apply.
- 장비 /picks/ (Read): recommended gear collected from posts, Coupang Partners links.

## What success looks like

The searcher gets the number they came for in the first screen (price, amount, deadline), trusts it because the source and check date sit next to it, and keeps reading into a related calculator, program or post.

## Constraints

- Static Astro on Cloudflare Pages; AdSense auto ads (page-level + bottom overlay) share the screen, so layouts must survive injected ad blocks.
- Coupang links: blog-tag href, banner image and alt verbatim; `rel="nofollow sponsored noopener"`, `referrerpolicy="unsafe-url"`.
- Facts only from official notices or cited press; illustrative values are labeled.
